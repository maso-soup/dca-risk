DCA Risk Dashboard

Long-horizon risk 0.0 (historically cheap) → 1.0 (historically expensive). Weekly close drives the signal.
How this works

Each component is scored point-in-time (no future data): its value is z-scored against an exponentially weighted history (≈4-year half-life, about one cycle) and mapped to 0–1. Cycle metrics carry most of the weight. The composite is evaluated at each weekly close; the zone only changes after 2 consecutive weekly closes in a new zone, so it won't flip-flop at boundaries.

Strong Buy <0.2: deploy 1/8 of cash reserve per week (≈2 months). Buy 0.2–0.4: deploy 1/20 per week (≈5 months). Hold 0.4–0.6: accumulate cash. Trim 0.6–0.8: sell 0–1% of holdings per week. Sell >0.8: sell 1% rising to 15% per week at 1.0. Can go to 100% cash.

Not financial advice. A model fit to a handful of cycles; past cycles may not repeat.
